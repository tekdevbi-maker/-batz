# What's New — v1.0.4

**Try before you sign up**
No account? No problem — create a free custom player card right from the app, no sign-in required.

**Download and print your card**
Every card — team player or guest — can now be downloaded as a print-ready PDF, sized for standard 2.5" x 3.5" trading cards.

**Smoother saving on Android**
Downloaded cards now save directly to your chosen folder instead of opening an app chooser.

**Stability improvements**
Fixed a crash affecting some iOS devices on launch, plus general bug fixes.
